1. The published site is responding: unauthenticated `GET /api/data-sync/player-stats-2026` returns **401**, not the prior 404. An unauthenticated agent cannot inspect the admin GET response directly, so its JSON source was not independently observed through that route. The deployed source-file ledger confirms the exact source below, and the published frontend bundle includes the matching source guard.
2. The production `nflverse_source_files` entry for `player_stats`, season 2026 (id 17) is **success**, with source `https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data/releases/download/stats_player/stats_player_week_2026.csv.gz`, 2,294 source rows reported, completed at 2026-09-25 19:42:21 UTC. Two `nflverse` sync-run entries (ids 537 and 538, 19:42:01 and 19:42:20 UTC) are **success**, each reporting 2,291 records processed. Those numbers have different ledger meanings; do not assume 2,294 inserted player-game records. No all-feed import was invoked by this verification.

Public `/api/consumer/player-usage` with `window=last3` returns season **2026** and two included completed team games for all four checks: SEA WR **partial** (Cooper Kupp, Rashid Shaheed, Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Montorie Foster Jr); SEA QB **partial** (Sam Darnold, Drew Lock); CLE RB **partial** (Quinshon Judkins, Dylan Sampson, Raheim Sanders, Jaleel McLaughlin); KC QB **available** (Patrick Mahomes). 4. Matchup-filtered SEA WR for game `401872955` returns only 2026 week 1 `401872656` and week 2 `401872943` (two included games). Production schedule identifies these as **NE at SEA** (final, September 10) and **SEA at ARI** (final, September 20); `401872955` is **SEA at WSH**, scheduled for September 27 at 17:00 UTC, and is excluded. The identifiers and opponent/week pair, not raw game-ID equality, support the match.

Zero-denominator derived metrics likewise remain unavailable. Restoration here means the verified 2026 player usage requests have data, **not** that every player has three games or every metric is supported.

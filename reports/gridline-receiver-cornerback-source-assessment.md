# Receiver–cornerback assignment evidence assessment

**Review date:** September 26, 2026. **Decision:** No assignment source is approved and usable for Game Detail today. Keep `directAssignments: null` and `projectedMatchups: []`; do not backfill a named matchup from depth, snaps, player box scores, unit statistics, or an analyst's expected alignment. This is a documentation and source-qualification review only: no authenticated/provider requests, paid calls, imports, production writes, model changes, or publication.

## What is needed, versus what exists

A receiver and a corner being on opposing rosters, or participating in the same game, is not evidence that the CB defended that WR. A pass defender on a *targeted play* is also not automatically the receiver's primary defender on every route. In zone coverage or on switches, a single pre-snap "associated defender" may not be an exclusive coverage assignment. Historical observed assignments and a *pregame projection* are different claims.

The [Game Detail investigation](gridline-game-detail-data-availability.md#individual-player-matchups-exact-evidence-gaps) documents the current production example: Houston at Indianapolis has player identities, recent defensive snaps and injury rows, but no direct assignment rows. `personnel-context-derivation.ts` explicitly leaves direct assignments null, `consumer.ts` emits an empty projection, and the UI shows the unavailable panel. The historical [readiness audit](gridline-current-state-audit.md#10-wr-cb-matchup-readiness) measured WR1/WR2 role coverage at 32/32 teams, CB1 at 26/32, CB2 at 28/32 and CB3/slot at 29/32; those are **role** counts from that audit, not assignment coverage or a current-season guarantee. Its Sleeper identity mapping was incomplete and included ambiguity/collisions. The source-backed **named WR–CB assignment count available to this route is zero**; no source-level eligible-pair denominator has been measured, so a coverage percentage would be invented.

## Acceptance gates before any future adoption

1. **Rights and definition first.** Obtain explicit permission to ingest, retain, derive from and display individual pair claims to Gridline users (including attribution/redistribution conditions), and a field-level definition distinguishing pre-snap association, target defender, primary route coverage, zone/shared coverage and editorial projection. A provider key or subscriber export is not permission. Do not make a paid trial call before approval.
2. **Immutable, point-in-time evidence.** Retain provider, dataset/version, source row/play ID, raw provider IDs, original source publication/update time, Gridline capture time, source content fingerprint and any corrected/superseded version. At a game-detail cutoff `min(request time, kickoff - 1ms)`, require **both** publication and capture at/before cutoff; for a saved prediction use its own earlier immutable evidence cutoff, never the later Game Detail cutoff. Exclude assignments observed in the selected game and later revised or season-end-released history from its pregame view. A future-game editorial prediction must carry its own published-at time; historical covered routes can only support an explicitly labeled forecast, not a verified future assignment.
3. **Unique identity and eligible coverage.** Resolve FTN/PFF/other player IDs to one WR and one CB on *opposing canonical teams* in the exact schedule game, with season/week, game-ID bridge and position checked as of cutoff. Reject ambiguous names, traded players, duplicates and crosswalk collisions. Audit mapped/ambiguous/unmatched pair counts; eligible routes/plays with both players and a meaningful pairing, pairs by game/team/WR/CB, missingness by week/alignment and provider revisions. Report numerator **and** eligible denominator, rather than zero-filling unknowns or reporting role/snap counts as assignment coverage. Validate representative man, zone, switch, and shadow examples with the provider's definitions before selecting thresholds.
4. **Injury and role freshness.** Join only pre-cutoff roster/depth and injury observations for **both** players, retaining source and observed-at times and an explicit fresh/stale/missing state. Missing injury rows are unknown, not “healthy”; a stale or out/doubtful designation must lower/suppress a projected pairing as appropriate. Do not let a current injury snapshot rewrite a historical forecast.
5. **Honest response contract and UI.** Preserve `basis: verified | inferred` and an explicit supported confidence (`high | medium | low`) with its rationale/sample, data freshness, source/cutoff and limitations for every pair. `verified` means an observed historical, source-attributed play/route association with the provider's documented definition, **not** an actually verified future assignment. Predicted future coverage must say `inferred`; never mark it verified because past pairs were observed. A partial game must not yield a blanket “this CB covers this WR” claim. Keep `projectedMatchups: []` and the unavailable UI where any required gate fails; the current OpenAPI matchup schema has names/summary only, and the UI's basis/confidence filter alone is not a provenance, injury or chronology validator. Specify and test the richer contract before any activation. No change to production models or confidence scores follows automatically from descriptive pair evidence.

**Recommendation:** Keep the existing unavailable state. If a properly licensed sample becomes available later, evaluate the FTN association definition and coverage/identity audit *offline* first, then compare any timestamped PFF projection separately as inferred evidence. Only build a user-facing projection after the gates above pass; do not lower them to fill the panel.

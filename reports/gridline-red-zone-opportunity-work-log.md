# Red Zone Opportunity release — development work log

## Initial audit (2026-09-25, before implementation)

- Gridline imports nflverse PBP, weekly player stats, snap counts and depth history through its existing `nflverse` sync. PBP is streamed into team-game and quarterback aggregates; individual play rows and receiver/rusher red-zone opportunities are **not persisted**. The existing team `red_zone_rate` does not support player opportunity claims. `player_game_stats` has season/game/team totals and receiving/rushing TD; `snap_counts` has offense snaps/pct, but PFR snap identifiers require the existing GSIS/PFR identity crosswalk. Game Detail and Player Usage already use same-season final games, a pre-kickoff cutoff, canonical team mappings and source-availability states. Receiving TD and a labeled period are already on Game Detail.
- NFLverse PBP `game_id` is source-specific (for example `2026_01_BUF_HOU`), not the ESPN schedule game ID. Match its season/week/teams to the canonical schedule before showing it for a game. PBP exposes `yardline_100`, `receiver_player_id`, `rusher_player_id`, `pass_attempt`, `rush_attempt`, `pass_touchdown`, `rush_touchdown`, `two_point_attempt`, `qb_kneel`, `qb_spike`, `qb_scramble` and `play_type`. A receiver ID on incomplete passes can be a credited target. Pass/run participation alone cannot establish a defensive matchup.
- Development has 2026 schedule with Weeks 1–2 final, 32 2026 team-game rows, 1,492 2026 snap rows, **no 2026 player-game-stat rows**. Its cached 2026 PBP is a September 17 download with 16 Week 1 games; the source ledger marks PBP successful (2,756 rows), player stats failed (no requested-season rows), and snap counts successful. Do not silently describe the cached Week 1 subset as current Week 1–2 coverage or as a fresh September 25 source. No provider refresh is required to inspect this cache.
- The existing consumer API explicitly marks `redZoneTargets`/`redZoneTouches` unsupported and does not have a red-zone endpoint or UI. Add an independently sourced derived game/player/zone layer and explicit freshness/coverage; do not relabel unsupported general Usage metrics until their contract and eligibility are changed. The new read path must work without a saved prediction or valid sportsbook market.
- Development schema is managed through the repository's Drizzle workflow; do not apply schema changes to production. Production schema updates are a separate user-initiated Publish concern. This release must not touch Phase 6.1 artifacts, predictions, backtests, production data or scheduled provider calls.

## Implementation and development verification

- Added additive, indexed player-game-zone and team-game-zone facts, a source-controlled development migration, and correction-safe derivation from nflverse PBP. The derivation uses pre-play `yardline_100 <= 20/10/5`, with overlapping thresholds; includes credited incomplete-pass targets and credited rush attempts (including QB scrambles); excludes two-point tries, kneels, spikes, nullified plays and missing player IDs. Team denominators use those same eligible credited attempts. Re-derivation validates its source before transactional replacement and leaves prior facts in place on empty/unusable input. Unknown upstream update time stays `null`; local ingestion time is recorded separately.
- Added a public read-only consumer endpoint, generated client and schemas, and desktop/mobile Red Zone dashboard. It offers season, team, position, inclusive zone and season/last-three-appearance filters, sortable observed totals/shares, verified snaps, source timestamps and explicit partial/unavailable states. A traded player's game-time team remains distinct, while last-three selection uses the player's latest completed appearances across teams. Game Detail's existing Player Usage cards retain their prior metrics and gain cutoff-safe red-zone figures. When player-stat cards are absent, a separately labeled red-zone-only fallback uses observed evidence instead of inventing general usage.
- Applied only the new development migration after a dry run. Re-derived from the **existing local 2026 PBP cache**; no provider request or scheduled job was run. A Washington abbreviation mismatch initially matched 15 games; after canonicalizing nflverse `WAS` to schedule `WSH`, all **16 cached Week 1 games** mapped. The run produced 1,761 player-zone facts and 96 team-zone facts. Cached 2025 PBP cannot be mapped because this development database has no corresponding 2025 schedule games; the CLI rejects that attempt without replacing good facts.
- The live development endpoint returned HTTP 200 with 587 players for the 2026 inside-20 season view and explicitly reported **16/32 completed games covered** (Week 2 missing), `sourceUpdatedAt: null`. For a Week 2 Detroit–Buffalo game cutoff, Detroit's last-three response included only the one covered prior completed game; it did not use the selected game. Jonathan Taylor's Week 1 inside-20 row reported five carries, two rushing TDs and five team carries, matching the observed play sample and team denominator. No prediction or market was required.
- API-server build, typecheck, 10 focused derivation/selection tests and 47 existing consumer route tests passed. The frontend typecheck, build and 15 focused presentation tests passed. Managed API and web workflows were restarted after clearing their stale port-owning processes; both are running. Desktop and mobile dashboard previews and a desktop/mobile Game Detail page rendered without a browser runtime error. Existing 2026 player-stat records remain absent, so the Game Detail preview uses the explicitly labeled red-zone-only fallback; production's populated cards still require post-publish verification.

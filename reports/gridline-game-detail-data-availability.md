## Player Usage: source-to-screen trace

1. **Schedule and completed games.** Read-only production `games` rows show IND–BAL `401872659` and HOU–BUF `401872660` in Week 1, HOU–CIN `401872934` and KC–IND `401872945` in Week 2; all four have `STATUS_FINAL` and precede Week 3 kickoff. There are **two eligible team games per side**, not five. A kickoff timestamp or a 0–0 score alone does not prove completion.
2. **Persisted NFLverse statistics.** Production `player_game_stats` contains 2026 `REG` rows for both teams' weeks 1–2. Examples: C.J. Stroud's passing attempts `38+55` and yards `274+353`; Daniel Jones's attempts include 31 in Week 1; Dalton Schultz has `8+14` targets; Xavier Hutchinson has `6+9` targets; Jonathan Taylor is in both weeks. Tyler Warren's `receiving_tds` is **1+1**. These are persisted source values, not inferred from a market or a score.
3. **Identity and team joins.** The consumer route converts ESPN schedule IDs to canonical `HOU`/`IND`, resolves season/week/team/opponent to eligible game IDs, and maps PFR snap IDs to GSIS stat player IDs using observed NFLverse identity rows. Production identity examples include Xavier Hutchinson `00-0038618` ↔ `HutcXa00`, Josh Downs `00-0038997` ↔ `DownJo00`, and Tyler Warren `00-0040128` ↔ `WarrTy00`. A bad or unknown identity must not silently contribute someone else's snaps. No identifier defect explains this matchup's current cards.
4. **Historical window and aggregation.** Game Detail applies the same-season, pre-cutoff, final-game list to stats and snaps, aggregates by **player and team** across the last five *team games*, then selects role-first QB/RB/WR/TE cards for each team. A missed player appearance stays partial rather than being replaced with an older game. Target share uses observed team targets; snap share uses joined offense snap percentages. Observed source zeros stay zero; unsupported red-zone touches/targets and explosive rate remain null, not `0`. No projection or valid sportsbook market is needed to build these cards.
5. **Actual published response and rendering.** `keyPlayers` contains HOU **C.J. Stroud, David Montgomery, Xavier Hutchinson, Dalton Schultz, Woody Marks** and IND **Daniel Jones, Jonathan Taylor, Josh Downs, Tyler Warren, Keenan Allen**. Stroud shows 93 attempts/627 pass yards, 5 carries/44 rush yards and 100% mean snap share; Montgomery 26 carries/70 rush yards, 5 targets/5 receptions/33 rec yards; Hutchinson 15 targets/69 rec yards; Schultz 22 targets/175 rec yards; Taylor 43 carries/190 rush yards, 8 targets/7 catches/63 rec yards. The published frontend groups by canonical team abbreviation and renders both columns of cards. “Status unconfirmed” is **player injury/roster eligibility**, not unavailable usage; the current `currentPersonnel.source` for these cards is “Current personnel evidence unavailable.” The two concepts must remain separate.

**Cutoff controls:** The already-completed SEA–ARI Week 2 game `401872943` returns pregame detail cards sourced only from Week 1 (Drew Lock **187**, not 422, passing yards; Jaxon Smith-Njigba **122**, not 277, receiving yards). The completed HOU–BUF Week 1 game `401872660` has **zero** same-season prior games and correctly has `keyPlayers: []` even though later 2026 and prior-2025 records exist. Its prediction is absent; that fact is independent of the honest no-usage result. Prior-season usage could be offered someday as a **separate, plainly labeled historical context**, with the player's identity, team and season verified; it cannot be silently spliced into current-season last-five averages or imply a current role. This repair deliberately does not add such a fallback.

**Source-backed metric scope:** QB passing attempts, completions, yards and TD plus rushing; RB carries/rush yards/targets/receptions/rec yards; WR/TE targets/receptions/rec yards and receiving TD; snap/target shares only where their denominators and identity joins exist. `totalTd` includes passing, receiving and rushing TD. The existing API exposed all except a **separate** receiving-TD metric; the development patch now sums `receivingTds`, preserves per-game null vs observed zero, includes it in the optional Game Detail contract, and labels it “Rec TD.” For Tyler Warren, the source-supported two-game expectation is **2 receiving TD**. The cards' header now names the **2026 season** and “up to 5 completed team games before Week 3.” When no eligible measurements exist, the empty message says no verified usage before the matchup instead of hinting that the database is globally empty.

## Individual Player Matchups: exact evidence gaps

- **Names are not assignments.** Production weeks 1–2 have HOU WRs Hutchinson and Nico Collins and CBs Derek Stingley Jr. and Kamari Lassiter; IND has WRs Josh Downs, Keenan Allen, Alec Pierce and CBs Sauce Gardner, Charvarius Ward, Justin Walley. The GSIS↔PFR mapping exists for sampled identities.
- **Participation is not coverage.** Read-only `snap_counts` shows HOU Stingley and Lassiter at 58/58 defensive snaps in Week 1 and 60/60 in Week 2; IND Gardner 68 snaps and Ward 65 in Week 1, and Gardner 76, Walley 68, Ward 36 in Week 2. This does not say which receiver they covered or verify the planned Week 3 roles.
- **Current depth and health remain uncertain.** The live context's current `wr1`/`wr2` roles are unconfirmed, and no verified WR/CB current depth rows were returned in the Game Detail context. IND injury evidence lists Ward **Questionable**, Pierce **Out**; HOU lists Collins **Doubtful**. Injury observations do not create a matchup assignment. The ten usage cards conservatively show eligibility **unknown** rather than claiming a current starter/available player.
- **Explicit evidence gates.** Personnel derivation sets `directAssignments: null` with the reason that immutable sources do not support direct player-vs-player assignments; the consumer response initializes `projectedMatchups` as `[]`. The frontend accepts only `basis: verified|inferred` **and** a non-unavailable confidence `high|medium|low`. It therefore correctly displays “Player matchup evidence unavailable.”
- **Existing defensive foundation:** `matchupBoard` already includes a separately labeled **Passing offense vs pass defense** team comparison, but this particular response marks it `insufficient`, with `Home 0 games · Away 0 games` and a requirement for at least **three** valid, cutoff-safe observations per side. Its **WR room vs secondary** category explicitly lacks coverage assignments and complete room efficiency. Do not substitute a QB-stability category or unrelated injury/snap percentages for individual CB-versus-WR statistics.

No new team-level panel is justified for this exact game; the existing team board already shows what is and is not supported. Do not lower confidence/sample or assignment gates merely to fill the Player Matchups section. The [receiver–cornerback source assessment](gridline-receiver-cornerback-source-assessment.md) evaluates public source documentation, licensing and point-in-time/identity gaps without provider calls; it does not approve a new feed or change this unavailable result.

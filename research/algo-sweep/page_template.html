Every algorithm predicted each game only from what was known before kickoff. They were ranked on 2023 and 2024, and then 2025 was used as an untouched test, so a model that got lucky in the first two seasons had to do it again to count.

The one result that survived

Bet the EPA rating's side at the opener when it disagrees by 4+ points

This showed up in the sweep as “50% EPA rating + 50% opening line, bet when 2+ points off the opener”, which is the same rule as a 4-point gap between the raw rating and the opener. Rules of this kind (bet only when a model blended with the opener is 2+ points off it) took the top four opener spots in 2023–24, and all four stayed above 58% in 2025: {{top4}}. It also matches the lead the earlier research found (lines move toward Gridline's number about two times in three), so it was not a fresh guess.

Why this is a lead and not a betting system yet

  • Luck check. On 2023–24 alone, the best of these rules had a {{top_adj}} chance of being luck once you account for trying {{n}} algorithms (sign-flip test), and the 4-point version {{s4_adj}}. The 2025 holdout is what makes it credible, and that is one season of about 50 bets.
  • QB timing. The rating knows who actually started at quarterback, which may not be known when the line opens. Games with no starter change still went {{s4_noqb}} ({{s4_noqb_pct}}), so the edge is not only QB news, but it is smaller.
  • 2026 is going the other way. {{s4_2026}} through three weeks. That is too few games to mean much, but it is the most recent evidence.
  • Price and availability. Grading assumes −110 at the exact opening number. Real openers move fast and limits are low.

Betting the same games at the closing number went {{s4_close}} ({{s4_close_pct}}). That was found after looking, not chosen in advance, so treat it as a question for next season rather than a result.

How it was tested

Method

  • Games: 2023, 2024 and 2025 regular season and playoffs (285 each), plus 48 games of 2026. Scores, closing spreads and weather come from nflverse. Opening spreads come from the public nfelo project.
  • Walk-forward: ratings use only earlier games. Anything fitted is fitted on 2020 through the season before.
  • Selection vs holdout: rankings use 2023–24 only. 2025 was not used to choose anything.
  • Luck bar: a sign-flip test reshuffles every game's cover outcome at once 4,000 times and asks how good the best of all {{n}} algorithms looks with zero skill. Typical: {{luck50}}; 95th percentile: {{luck95}}.
  • Families swept: Elo (K, margin-of-victory, regression, home field), point-differential ratings (half-life, carryover, caps), the EPA rating across 14 decay and QB-shrinkage builds, feature subsets, regularization, gradient boosting, random forests and other learners, fixed home-field values, rest and bye handling, QB effect sizes, wind and cold, blends with the opener, market-aware models, ensembles and 29 classic betting rules.
  • Caveats: weather is the kickoff reading, not the forecast. QB features use the player who actually started. Records assume −110 on every bet.

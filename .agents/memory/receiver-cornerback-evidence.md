---
name: Receiver–cornerback evidence boundary
description: Source-qualification rules for individual coverage claims and pregame projections.
---

Do not equate a documented pre-snap associated defender, a target's pass defender, a primary route coverage assignment, or an editorial expected opponent. Each supports a different claim. Public participation/player stats and roster depth do not establish a named pair. A postgame or season-end release is not pregame evidence for that game's forecast.

**Why:** Public vendor catalogs can describe commercial fields that are absent from freely released datasets, and a historical observed association does not verify a future assignment. License, mapping and release chronology are separate gates.

**How to apply:** For any future matchup feed, qualify contractual display rights, field meaning, unique player/game/team mapping, source publication and capture times, pair-level coverage denominators, and both players' injury freshness before exposing it. Label future projections inferred even when based on verified past observations; keep unsupported pairs unavailable.

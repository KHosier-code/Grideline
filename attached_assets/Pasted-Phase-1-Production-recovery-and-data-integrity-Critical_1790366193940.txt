What we learned from the models
We should use the existing results to guide development without overinterpreting them.

Previously retained results also showed the recorded market had lower forecast error than these models on the compared games.

Another important finding is that the historical evaluation reported no usable historical injury, offensive-line or secondary source evidence for its 554 team observations. We therefore haven't properly established whether those particular personnel features would improve Gridline.
That's why our next analytical investment should focus on verified historical datasets, rather than adding more variables indiscriminately.

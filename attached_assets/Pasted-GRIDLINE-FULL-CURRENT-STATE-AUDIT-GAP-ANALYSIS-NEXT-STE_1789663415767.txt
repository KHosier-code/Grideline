GRIDLINE — FULL CURRENT-STATE AUDIT, GAP ANALYSIS, NEXT-STEP RECOMMENDATIONS, AND EXTERNAL PRODUCT REVIEW

Goal:
Produce the most detailed, evidence-based assessment possible of the current Gridline project AND prepare a safe way for an external reviewer to inspect the consumer experience.

This is primarily a READ-ONLY AUDIT.

Do NOT:
- change model logic
- retrain or refit models
- promote models
- rewrite predictions
- alter sportsbook history
- weaken authentication
- expose admin functionality publicly
- expose secrets
- expose private database controls
- expose raw provider payloads
- publish unverified experimental functionality into production

The purpose is to answer:

1. What is Gridline today?
2. What is actually working?
3. What data do we truly have?
4. What analytics are genuinely implemented versus only displayed?
5. What is missing?
6. Where are the biggest weaknesses?
7. What should be built next?
8. Which parts are production-ready versus still experimental?
9. Is the current product analytically strong enough to justify its premium-looking UI?
10. What would materially improve prediction quality, player analytics, and betting research value?
11. What does the actual consumer product feel like when reviewed as a user rather than as a developer?

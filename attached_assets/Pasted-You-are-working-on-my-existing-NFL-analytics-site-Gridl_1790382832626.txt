## Data and calculation rules

Use verified nflverse play by play for play location and the credited receiver or rusher. Use existing verified player stats and snap counts where they provide a better authoritative cross-check. Add only the ingestion and schema changes the audit shows are necessary.

- “Inside the 20,” “inside the 10,” and “inside the 5” refer to the **ball’s position before the play**: `yardline_100 <= 20`, `<= 10`, or `<= 5`, respectively. These are overlapping ranges; label them clearly.
- Count a **target** when a specific player is credited as the intended receiver, including an incomplete pass. Count a **carry** when a player is credited as the rusher. Follow source definitions for scrambles, and document how they are treated.
- Count receiving and rushing touchdowns separately. Exclude two-point tries, kneeldowns, spikes, nullified plays, and plays without a reliably identified player from the corresponding opportunity count.
- Calculate team target share as player targets divided by **all qualifying team targets in the same zone and period**. Calculate carry share using qualifying team carries. If a denominator is zero, display “—” rather than 0%.
- Group by the player’s team **at the time of the game**, so trades do not misattribute historical opportunities.
- “Last three” means that player’s last three **completed games in which they appeared**, within the selected regular season. Show the number of games actually available if fewer than three.
- Keep regular season and postseason separate. Never include a future game or an unfinished game in observed totals.
- Do not infer a cornerback assignment, coverage shell, or player matchup from participation on the same play.

Validate aggregates against source play rows and, where comparable, official player touchdown totals. Investigate discrepancies instead of silently adjusting the numbers.

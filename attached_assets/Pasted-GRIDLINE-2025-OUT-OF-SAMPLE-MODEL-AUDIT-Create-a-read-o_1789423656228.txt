GRIDLINE 2025 OUT-OF-SAMPLE MODEL AUDIT

Create a read-only audit of Gridline's 2025 model performance.

Do not retrain, refit, promote, modify, or generate any model.

Do not fabricate historical sportsbook lines, predictions, grades, or CLV.

The purpose is to determine exactly how the models that were evaluated out-of-sample in 2025 performed across the full season AND week by week.

IMPORTANT

2025 must remain true out-of-sample evaluation.

Use only model runs where:
- training data ends before the 2025 test games
- 2025 games were not used to fit the evaluated model
- feature chronology is valid
- no future information leaked into predictions

PART 1 — FULL 2025 SEASON SUMMARY

For each model family report:

SPREAD
- algorithm/model version
- games evaluated
- MAE
- RMSE
- mean prediction error/bias
- median absolute error
- games within 3 points of actual margin
- games within 7 points of actual margin
- directional winner accuracy
- ATS wins/losses/pushes and ATS percentage ONLY if legitimate historical sportsbook spread data exists
- do not reconstruct or fabricate spreads

MONEYLINE
- algorithm/model version
- games evaluated
- correct winners
- incorrect winners
- win-prediction accuracy
- log loss
- Brier score
- calibration if available
- favorites versus underdogs accuracy
- home versus away accuracy

TOTALS
- algorithm/model version
- games evaluated
- MAE
- RMSE
- mean total prediction error/bias
- median absolute error
- games within 3 points of actual total
- games within 7 points of actual total
- over/under wins/losses/pushes ONLY if legitimate historical total lines exist
- do not fabricate totals

Clearly separate:

A. MODEL PREDICTION ACCURACY
from
B. BETTING RECORD

A projected winner being correct is not automatically a profitable moneyline bet.

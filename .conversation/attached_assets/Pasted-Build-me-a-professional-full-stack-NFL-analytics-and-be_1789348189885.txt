## WALK-FORWARD BACKTESTING

Avoid training models using information that would not have existed at prediction time.

When backtesting Week 10 of a season, only allow the algorithm access to information available before Week 10.

Never leak:

* final score
* closing line
* future injury information
* future depth charts
* future statistics

into the training data.

Use chronological/walk-forward validation rather than random train/test splits for primary model evaluation.
